# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here. In issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Keep private information private, yours and other people's.
- Stay on topic, and be patient with newcomers.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block people who don't follow these rules. To
raise a concern privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
